Fabrikam Analytics sells revenue dashboards to B2B sales and RevOps teams. Every name, number and link in this example is fictional.

The homepage states the value clearly and offers two paths (demo and free trial), and the company invests in search: Semrush models a small paid search budget next to a larger organic base.

The weak spots are public reputation and social proof: few reviews, one review site below average, and no customer stories linked from the homepage.

Conclusão executiva

Fabrikam has real foundations: a clear offer, a working site, search investment and an active LinkedIn presence.

The two gaps that slow growth are thin public proof and an unanswered complaint trail on one review site.

Start with reputation: answer every open complaint and collect G2 reviews from happy accounts, then add proof next to the demo button.
